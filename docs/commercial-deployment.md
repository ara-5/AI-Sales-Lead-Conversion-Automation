# Commercial deployment guide (draft)

All prices below are estimates to validate with real pilots and current vendor pricing. None come from completed sales.

## Per-client setup checklist
1. Create the client's own Zapier workspace/account or use a client-owned connection. Do not share your credentials.
2. Copy the template Sheet; set business name, type, market, categories, score thresholds, working hours.
3. Connect the client's Gmail/Workspace, CRM and Slack through their own authorization.
4. Set the approval channel and the approver. Keep approval ON for the first month.
5. Run the synthetic test set before any real lead goes through.
6. Confirm consent and privacy wording on the client's form (UAE PDPL and any client-region rules; get legal review).

## Cost items to check
- Zapier plan (multi-step Zaps, Paths, webhooks, Code and scheduled Zaps may need paid tiers) and task usage per lead.
- AI usage (Zapier AI credits or your own API key).
- HubSpot or other CRM seats, Calendly paid features, Slack workspace.

## Pilot offer (to validate)
A 30-day pilot for one lead source and one team: setup, testing and weekly tuning, with approval kept on.

## Support
Monitor failed runs, review AI categorization monthly, adjust prompts.
